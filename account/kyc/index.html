Why KYC Verification?

Know Your Customer (KYC) verification is a mandatory process that helps us verify your identity and ensure compliance with anti-money laundering (AML) regulations. This process protects both you and Beta Concept from potential fraud and ensures a secure investment environment.

Your documents are processed securely and stored in compliance with GDPR and international data protection standards.

Required Documents

To complete your verification, please prepare the following documents:

• Identity Document: Valid passport, national ID card, or driver's license.
• Proof of Address: Utility bill, bank statement, or official correspondence (dated within 3 months).
• Selfie Verification: A clear photo of yourself holding your ID document.
